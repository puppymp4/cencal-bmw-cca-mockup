Chapter notes  ·  No. 1

The valley's BMW club meets on the second Saturday of every month.

Coffee and cars at Woodward Park, a European show at Clovis Community College every June, and instructed track days at Buttonwillow. Members drive everything from an E30 kept since the eighties to a 3 Series bought last month. All makes are welcome at the meets.

The season

Four dates, announced each year
I.

Euro Car Show

The chapter's own show at Clovis Community College. Awards across categories, open to European makes, run by the board and volunteers.

June$20 entryOpen to the public
II.

High Performance Driving School

Two days of instructed lapping at Buttonwillow with the San Diego chapter. Classroom, an assigned instructor, your own car.

SpringMembers only
III.

Legends of the Autobahn

The national German-car concours during Monterey Car Week, at Pacific Grove Golf Links. Members caravan over from the valley.

AugustMembers only
IV.

The Toy Drive

The last Kars & Koffee of the year. Same place, same time, and everyone brings a toy for Valley Children's Hospital.

Membership

Subscribe to the club.

You do not need to join to come to a meet. Membership is what gets you onto the track, into Legends of the Autobahn, and a copy of Roundel in the mailbox every month.

  • Roundel magazine, monthly
  • Driving school at Buttonwillow Raceway
  • Legends of the Autobahn, Monterey Car Week
  • Chapter events across the Central Valley
Join through BMW CCA
$58per year, paid to BMW CCA national

Pick Central California as your chapter at signup. There is no separate chapter fee.
