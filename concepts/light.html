Membership

You do not need to join to show up.

Membership is what gets you onto the track, into Legends of the Autobahn, and Roundel magazine in the mailbox every month. It is paid to BMW CCA national. Pick Central California as your chapter.

  • Roundel magazine, monthly
  • Driving school at Buttonwillow Raceway
  • Legends of the Autobahn, Monterey Car Week
  • Chapter events across the Central Valley
