Join the chapter

Straight answer first: you do not need a membership to come to Kars & Koffee, and you do not need one to be welcome here. Membership is what unlocks the track and the national events.

What it costs

$58 a year, paid to BMW CCA national

BMW CCA is a national club with chapters all over the country. You join once, at the national level, and pick Central California as your chapter. Your dues then support what happens here.

There is no separate chapter fee. There is no application to be reviewed. You sign up, and the next Roundel shows up in your mailbox.

How to do it

  1. Go to the BMW CCA membership page.
  2. Create an account and pay the $58 annual dues.
  3. When asked for a chapter, choose Central California.
  4. Follow @bmwccacencal so you catch the event announcements.
  5. Come to the next Kars & Koffee. That part is free either way.

What you actually need a card for

Free, no membership

  • Kars & Koffee, every second Saturday
  • Bimmers & Bagels, Bimmers & Bros, Bimmers at the Lake
  • The December toy drive
  • Bringing any make, any model, any condition

Members only

  • The driving school at Buttonwillow Raceway
  • Legends of the Autobahn at Pacific Grove
  • Roundel magazine
  • Club corrals at national race weekends

Track events run under BMW CCA insurance, which is the reason for the membership requirement. It is not a gate the chapter put up.
