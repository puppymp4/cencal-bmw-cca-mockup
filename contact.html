Before you come

The questions we get every month.

Do I need a BMW to come to Kars & Koffee?

No. All makes are welcome at the monthly meet. Plenty of regulars drive something else entirely, and that has never been a problem.

Do I need to be a BMW CCA member?

Not for Kars & Koffee or the other casual meets. Membership is required for the driving school at Buttonwillow and for Legends of the Autobahn, because those run under BMW CCA insurance.

Where exactly is the monthly meet?

The Starbucks at 9423 N Fort Washington Rd, Fresno, across from Woodward Park. Second Saturday, 8 AM to noon. People start rolling in a little before eight and it thins out around eleven.

Is there a cost?

Kars & Koffee is free. The Euro Car Show has a $20 entry if you are showing a car. Track days and Monterey are priced per event.

How do I buy a chapter shirt?

$25 in S through XL and $30 in XXL. Ask at any Kars & Koffee, or message the chapter on Instagram.

I am new to the area. Is it worth showing up alone?

Yes. Park, get a coffee, and walk the row. Asking somebody about their car is the entire social protocol here.
